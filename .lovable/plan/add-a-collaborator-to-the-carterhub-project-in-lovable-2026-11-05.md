# Add a collaborator to the CarterHub project in Lovable

No app changes are needed. CarterHub stays private to you, so the collaborator can help build it but cannot sign in to your live workspace.

## Steps (about 2 minutes)
1. Click **Share** at the top right of the editor.
2. Enter their email address.
3. Pick **Editor** if they should make changes, or **Viewer** if they should only look.
4. Send the invite. They'll get an email.

## Good to know
- They will only see this project, not your other projects.
- Anything they build uses your workspace's credits.
- To give them access to all your projects instead, go to **Settings → People**.
- You can remove them or change their role later from the same Share menu.
